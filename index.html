Server #627: How We Defend the Capital

SVS Capital Clash. Defence only. Please read it all. It is short.

1. Why this guide is only about defence

If we are defending, we lost invasion rights. This means the invading server is stronger than us. We cannot win by fighting them one to one. We must win by working together better than they do.

2. Who defends

The top six alliances defend the Capital. On #627 we have:

  • 2 very strong alliances with T10 units.
  • 4 alliances of similar strength with many level 30 and 31 players, but no T10 units.

3. Territory is force

We win by showing force. We can only show force where we control territory. If we hold the ground, we can rally and reinforce quickly. If the enemy holds the ground, they can do the same to us.

4. The result is decided before the fight

The Capital and the three turrets are open to attack from 12:00 to 14:00 server time. But most of the result is decided before 12:00.

We already know how to occupy the territory. We know how to use season buildings to limit the free space. The real question is: how well can the enemy land and expand? Invaders can teleport in from 00:30 server time. Every gap in the mud is a place where they can land. If they land and grow, they control territory, and then they can show force at 12:00.

Orange: the landing and expanding phase. Red: the open battle. Time is server time.

5. The enemy will attack our weakest point

The enemy is not stupid. They will target the weakest alliance and the weakest bases to make a beachhead. If we do not work together, the battle becomes our weakest alliance against their strongest alliance. We will lose that battle.

What we will do

A. Turn their plan into our advantage

We know where they will try to land. So we put our two strongest alliances near the likely breach point. When the enemy lands there, our strongest players can respond fast.

B. One War Room chat

We will make a new War Room chat. It is active from reset until the battle is over. It is only for the battle.

The existing NAP chat stays for admin topics only. Do not use it during the battle.

C. Two roles

C.1 The General

The General has one goal: the enemy never gets a foothold.

When the battle starts at 12:00, there should be zero enemy bases on the mud.

When an enemy base lands on the mud, we attack it at once. We do not wait. We do not let it grow.

  • The General should not be our strongest player. We need our strongest players fighting.
  • The General does not fight. Their job is to watch the whole battlefield and stop every landing as fast as possible.
  • A tablet or PC is best. A bigger screen shows more of the map.
  • When The General calls a rally, it comes before everything else.

C.2 Area Commander

  • One person from each alliance, usually an R4.
  • Each Area Commander leads one area of the map.
  • Inside their area, they decide what to do. The General gives the overall direction.

D. Timing: the most important point

These roles must be filled from reset until the battle ends. That is more than 13 hours. No one person can do this alone. So we need a roster with shifts for The General and for each Area Commander.

If you take a shift, you commit to being online and ready for the whole shift. Before your shift ends, hand over to the next person in the War Room.
